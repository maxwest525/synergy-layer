# Brand and Claims

> AOOS may reorganize verified facts; it may not invent the facts it needs.

## Claim classes

| Class                        | Required support                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity/contact             | authoritative business record and current owned-site confirmation               |
| Service/coverage             | approved operations record and exact geographic/service scope                   |
| Price/savings                | current calculation, assumptions, effective date, and required qualification    |
| Performance/result           | first-party measurement with cohort/window and non-causal wording unless tested |
| Credential/license/insurance | authoritative issuing record and validity date                                  |
| Review/testimonial           | traceable source, permission, exact attribution, and no material alteration     |
| Comparative/superlative      | defensible current comparison and compliance approval                           |

## Prohibited claims

No fabricated credentials, reviews, service areas, availability, prices, guarantees, rankings, safety claims, carrier quality, savings, timelines, customer counts, or competitor comparisons. No generated statistic may support itself. No DataForSEO, AI, or generic tool score becomes an E-E-A-T, authority, traffic, or business-outcome claim.

## Drafting rule

If a proposal needs a fact not present in the rendered page or an approved claim record, reject the proposal or remove the fact. `Unknown` is a valid result. Demo data is prohibited unless the operator explicitly requests it and it is visibly labeled.

## Current state

The repo does not contain a verified, complete TruMove brand-and-claims registry. Therefore this file defines the guardrail but does not invent company facts. Operational claim activation is `BLOCKED` on an approved authoritative export.

# Products outside the loop

**Date:** 2026-09-02. **Status:** ideas to explore, none started. Each one that
is worth pursuing carries an `IDEA-n` row in
[`docs/context/BACKLOG.md`](../../context/BACKLOG.md) section G; that row, not
this file, says whether it is still open.

"The loop" is the page-change lifecycle: a finding drafts a change, the change
sits at `/changes/$id`, an operator approves it, `runTransition` applies it to
the website repository and a measurement window judges it. Everything below
lives **outside** that loop: it reads, observes, compares or alerts, and never
writes to the website. That is what makes these cheap to add. None of them
needs a new change kind, a new executor path or a new approval state, and none
of them needs the seventh navigation slot: every one goes inside a category
that already exists.

Evidence grades follow `.claude/skills/seo-measurement/SKILL.md`: `[G]` Google
or the provider's own documentation, `[P]` published method, `[V]` vendor
content, `[computed]` with the method stated. A volume figure is `[V]` and is
shown as an estimate, never as a count.

## The operator's idea: a continuous AEO loop feeding the resource center

Restated by the operator on 2026-09-02, after the list above. It absorbs
IDEA-3, which was the first read of it and nothing more. This is the one the
operator wants expanded and built, so it gets more room than the rest, and it
is the one that needs a spec and provider digests before code.

**What exists.** The public site carries an "ask about us on Claude" control
that opens a Claude session with a prepared prompt and shows the visitor what
the model answers. The site also carries a resource center at `/research`:
four posts today, served from a content table, prerendered as an empty hub
(SEO-1, website repository). Both are the seed of what follows.

**What the operator described.** Four parts, which are four products, and one
of them lives inside the loop.

**How the four run as one loop.** Ask (IDEA-24) and harvest (IDEA-25) file
gaps. Data (IDEA-26) turns a gap into a draft with citations: the answer to the Reddit thread or the model's question, published on the owned site as the piece a future search or answer engine cites. The operator
approves a batch. The site publishes. Ask runs again and the stored answers
show whether the brand is now named, cited, and described correctly. Every
stage writes rows the next one reads, which is the standing check the backlog
ends with: a collector with its consumer in the same change.

## Declined on purpose

Not listed above because doctrine already answers them.

- **Google Business Profile, local pack, review platforms, citations, city
  radius pages.** Out of scope for a nationwide broker (COMPETITIVE_MODEL §1).
- **A social posting scheduler.** Social is not a documented ranking signal
  (small-site research §2). Measure the channel first (IDEA-7); a scheduler is a
  separate product decision after that.
- **Email nurture or lead follow-up.** The README says what this is not: a CRM.
- **Forecasting, projected traffic, projected revenue.** No forecasting layer
  exists and an invented projection was the AGT-3 finding.
- **Link buying, outreach automation, guest-post marketplaces.** Never
  recommended (small-site research §3).
- **Anything that creates or edits a page.** That is the loop. Route pages
  (CONTENT-5) are the biggest item there and IDEA-1 and IDEA-15 exist to size
  them.
